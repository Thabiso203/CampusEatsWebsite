We're here to help

Need a hand ordering?

Use this quick guide if you're stuck choosing items, checking out, or following an order.

1. Choose your food

Open the menu, search for a dish, and use the item controls to add the quantity you want to your cart.

2. Check your cart

Open your cart, review the items and quantities, then continue to checkout. If the cart is empty, return to the menu and add an item first.

3. Place your order

Enter your delivery details, choose an available payment method, and review the order before submitting it. Campus Eats does not take card payments on this site.

4. Track your order

Sign in and open My Orders. Select Track Order to see the latest status. Keep the page open for automatic status updates.

Can I cancel?

You can cancel while an order is still Pending. Once staff accept it, the cancellation option is removed. If you already made a bank transfer for a cancelled order, contact staff.

Something went wrong?

Try refreshing the page and checking your internet connection. Make sure you're signed in before opening My Orders. If checkout still fails, avoid submitting the same order repeatedly.

Still stuck?

If you cannot place an order or your order details look incorrect, contact the Campus Eats staff through the contact channel provided by your food vendor. Include your order ID if you already have one, but never share your password.
